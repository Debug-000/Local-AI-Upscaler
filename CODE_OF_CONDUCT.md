# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Be respectful, direct, and constructive.

## Expected Behavior

- Assume good intent.
- Give actionable feedback.
- Focus criticism on the work, not the person.
- Keep discussions professional and inclusive.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove contributions or discussions that violate these expectations.
